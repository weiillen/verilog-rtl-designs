# Portfolio Scope

## Included

- the student's original Homework 4 submission report;
- the student's original Homework 6 submission report;
- simulation-preview images derived from those reports; and
- documentation describing the submitted architectures and verification evidence.

## Excluded

- the two assignment-specification PDFs supplied by the course;
- `.DS_Store` files;
- `__MACOSX` resource-fork files; and
- other archive metadata.

The assignment specifications were used only to understand the intended design objectives. They were excluded because they are course materials rather than the student's own portfolio work.
